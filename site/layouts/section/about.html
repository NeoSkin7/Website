Who I Am

I am a student of Royal Holloway, University of London, studying to become a Master of Science (MSci) in Theoretical Physics. I am dual-located in Egham and Poole, United Kingdom, but have travelled far and wide during my globe-trotting holidays. Whilst my main academic interest is in physics, my relentless curiosity also extends to mathematics, chemistry, and the environment, where I explore and adventure to discover the secrets of the universe. However, everyone has to have (at least a few) hobbies, and I spend my remaining time coin collecting, hiking, and film watching.

What I Do

This website is my digital home and where I publish the things I am proud (and no so proud) of. Here, you will find:

Articles

My rule for writing articles (or blog posts) are that they have to be interesting. Whilst that is quite an open brief, I generally write articles about either: (a) key issues and news stories, or (b) STEM topics - science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. I try to make my articles easy to understand for everyone, regardless of whether I am writing about rocket launches or climate change, or anything in-between, as I believe that knowledge and information should be open and accessible.
